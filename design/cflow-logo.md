# CFlow Logo

## Concept

The mark reduces CFlow to one continuous route. The filled dot is the workflow
input and the open chevron is the output; the path rises through a compact,
counter-clockwise turn. The geometry stays legible at the 34px size used by the
workbench.

## Palette

- Background: `#171A1F`
- Line and wordmark: `#F8FAFC`
- Lockup inset: `#23272E`

## Usage

- Use `web/public/cflow-mark.svg` for the app icon, favicon, and compact product UI.
- Use `web/public/cflow-lockup.svg` when the product name needs to be visible.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its width.
- Do not recolor individual parts, add shadows, or place the mark on a similarly
  dark background without retaining its square container.
